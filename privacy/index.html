Privacy Policy

Applies to: Graveyard Merge: Idle Monsters (Android) and other games published by LionClad Studios.
Effective date: October 1, 2026

The short version: we don't ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress is saved only on your device. The game shows optional ads through Unity LevelPlay, and those ad partners may collect device information such as your advertising ID to show and measure ads. Purchases are paid through Google Play and confirmed through Unity In-App Purchasing.

Who we are

LionClad Studios ("we", "us") is an independent game developer. This policy explains what information our games handle and why.

Information we collect ourselves

None that identifies you. Our games do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details. We do not run our own servers that receive information about you.

Your game progress (for example your creatures, upgrades and settings such as sound and vibration) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free to play and shows optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the advertising networks it works with.

To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, these providers may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (on Android, the Google Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was viewed or tapped).

This information is collected and used by those providers under their own privacy policies. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for how Unity and its partners handle it, and for how to exercise your rights with them.

Your choices: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). Where the law requires consent for personalized ads (for example in the EU, UK and certain US states), the game will ask for or honor that choice.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google handles your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy.

The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to talk to Google Play and confirm purchases. To do that, Unity processes your purchase history and receipts, device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic and performance data; information on a receipt (such as an email address) may be processed briefly to verify it and is not kept. This is handled under Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. The game itself only learns which item was bought so it can unlock it.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Because your progress lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the game. We hold no other data about you to delete. For information collected by ad providers, use the device settings above or contact the provider through its privacy policy.

Security

We keep data collection to a minimum. Information exchanged with ad and purchase providers is sent over encrypted connections by their software.

Changes to this policy

If our games start handling information differently, we will update this page and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: email lioncladstudios@gmail.com.
